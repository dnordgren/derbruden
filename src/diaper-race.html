How this works

Standings count only regular-season games (playoffTierType NONE). Wins + 0.5×ties sorted, then points for — the diaper goes to the worst record with lower PF as the tiebreak. Doomed means even winning all remaining games, the team still finishes last even with tiebreak help. Safe means even losing out, the team cannot finish last even losing tiebreaks. Enumeration tries all 2n outcomes for the 65 remaining regular games (capped at 20); head-to-head conflicts are respected, so not every team can win out simultaneously. Future points are ignored for tiebreak — wins grouping is conservative. Hypotheticals show Monday-night "hold vs flip" for the current in-progress week only.
