How this works

Standings count only regular-season games (playoffTierType NONE). Wins + 0.5×ties sorted, then points for. Clinched means even losing all remaining games, the team still finishes top 6 even if every tied team wins the tiebreak. Eliminated means even winning out, the team cannot reach top 6 even with tiebreak help. Enumeration tries all 2n outcomes for the 65 remaining regular games (capped at 20); head-to-head conflicts are respected, so not every team can win out simultaneously. Future points are ignored for tiebreak — wins grouping is conservative. Hypotheticals show Monday-night "hold vs flip" for the current in-progress week only.

Data from the ESPN fantasy API. Regenerate with make playoff or wait for the Monday night GitHub Action. Page and JSON refresh together; start week is 11 (regular season is 13 weeks).
