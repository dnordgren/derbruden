How this works

Standings count only regular-season games (playoffTierType NONE). Wins + 0.5×ties sorted, then points for. Clinched means even losing all remaining games, the team still finishes top 6 even if every tied team wins the tiebreak. Eliminated means even winning out, the team cannot reach top 6 even with tiebreak help. Same math at the bottom: doomed means even winning out leaves the team last even with tiebreak help, safe means even losing out cannot finish last — worst record takes the diaper, lower points for breaks ties. Enumeration tries all 2n outcomes for the 25 remaining regular games (capped at 20); head-to-head conflicts are respected, so not every team can win out simultaneously. Future points are ignored for tiebreak — wins grouping is conservative. PO% is the share of all remaining-game outcomes (a coin flip each; tied teams split the contested spots) that puts the team in the top 6; D% the share that finishes last. Past 20 remaining games a seeded 20,000-sample Monte Carlo estimates the same shares deterministically. Hypotheticals show Monday-night "hold vs flip" for the current in-progress week only.
