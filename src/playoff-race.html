How this works

Only regular-season games count. Teams sort by wins plus half wins for ties, then points for. A team has clinched if it finishes top 6 even when it loses out and every tiebreak goes against it. A team is eliminated if it misses the top 6 even when it wins out and every tiebreak goes its way. The same math sets the diaper race at the bottom. A team is doomed when it finishes last even when it wins out. It is safe when it cannot finish last even when it loses out. The math tries every outcome of the remaining games. Head-to-head results carry over, so rivals cannot all win out at once. Future points do not break ties. PO% is the share of all remaining-game outcomes that puts a team in the top 6. D% is the share that finishes last. Monday-night hypotheticals show what happens if leaders hold or underdogs rally.

Scenarios update Monday nights. The page and JSON refresh together.
